Presenter controls

Allow new sign-ups

Turn off to stop anyone new from creating an account. People who already have one can still sign in.

Devices allowed per account

How many devices one person can be signed in on at once. An extra sign-in signs the oldest device out.

Maintenance lock

Locks the presenter screen for everyone except administrators, with the message below. Anything already on the projector keeps showing.

Message to everyone

Shows a dismissible notice inside the presenter for everyone who is signed in.

Sign out everyone

Immediately signs every person out on every device. They can sign straight back in. This also signs you out of the presenter if you have it open.
